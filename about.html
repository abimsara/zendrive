About ZenDrive

Sri Lanka's trusted car rental partner. Driven by quality, powered by service.

Our Story

Years of Trust, Quality & Service

ZenDrive was founded with a simple mission: to offer Sri Lankans a premium car rental experience at fair prices, with transparent terms and zero hidden fees.

Starting from a small office in Colombo, we've grown to become one of Sri Lanka's most trusted mobility partners. Today we operate multiple convenient locations, maintain a fleet of premium vehicles, and serve thousands of happy customers each year.

Whether you need a compact car for the city, an SUV for a family trip, or a luxury vehicle for a special occasion, we have the right car — and the right service — for you.
